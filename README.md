## 📌 Problem Statement

Many people in India do not know their fundamental rights when interacting with the police. In high-stress situations (street checks, traffic stops, searches, FIR refusals, or arrests), citizens often lack immediate clarity on:
- What the police are legally authorized to do vs. what exceeds their power.
- What immediate steps they should take to protect themselves.
- What actions they must avoid to prevent self-incrimination or escalation.
- Where and how to file official complaints if rights are infringed.

**Know Your Police Rights** bridges this gap by presenting authoritative legal guidance in a **7-step structured format** accessible within **30 seconds** on any mobile or desktop browser.

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### ⚡ 1. 30-Second Crisis SOS Mode
- Prominent high-contrast modal for active police interactions (e.g. stopped on street, house search, detention).
- Displays the **3 Golden Rules** immediately.
- Integrated **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice Narration** (English & Hindi) for hands-free audio playback during stress.
- One-tap direct dialers for national emergency helplines:
  - `112` National Emergency
  - `181` Women Helpline
  - `1064` Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB)
  - `15100` NALSA Free Legal Aid
  - `14449` Cybercrime Helpline

### 📚 2. Complete 12 Police Scenario Guides
Comprehensive guides covering all core police interaction scenarios:
1. **Police Stop or Questioning on Street / Traffic**
2. **Arrest & Custodial Detention**
3. **Police Refusal to Register an FIR**
4. **Personal, Vehicle, or Home Search**
5. **Smartphone, Digital Device, or Property Seizure**
6. **Police Misconduct, Assault, or Physical Abuse**
7. **Police Officer Demanding a Bribe**
8. **Women & Police Interaction Safeguards**
9. **Detained for an Extended Period (Beyond 24 Hours)**
10. **Police Inaction / Refusal of Assistance**
11. **Police Notice Received (Sec 35 BNSS / 41A CrPC)**
12. **Police Home Visit for Investigation**

Every scenario provides a **7-Step Clarity Breakdown**:
1. *What is Happening?*
2. *Your Legal Rights (Citing BNSS 2023, CrPC 1973, BNS & SC Rulings)*
3. *What You Should Do Right Now*
4. *What You Should Avoid Doing*
5. *Where & How to Complain*
6. *Evidence & Documents to Retain*
7. *Next Legal Remedy*

### ✍️ 3. Official SP / PCA Complaint Letter Generator
- Interactive legal document generator creating formal representations under **Section 173(4) BNSS 2023 / Section 154(3) CrPC**.
- Inputs: Complainant details, Target Police Station, Officer Name/Badge Number, Incident Date, and Factual Statement.
- Instant live legal preview with one-click **Copy Text** and **Print / Export PDF**.

### 🏛️ 4. State Police Complaints & Help Directory
- Directory covering **28 Indian States & Union Territories** (Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Gujarat, Telangana, Kerala, Rajasthan, etc.).
- Direct contact numbers, addresses, and official web portals for:
  - State Police Complaints Authority (PCA)
  - Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB)
  - State Human Rights Commission (SHRC)
  - District Legal Services Authority (DLSA / NALSA)

### 🤖 5. Nyaya AI Interactive Legal Assistant & Website Guide
- Floating interactive chatbot launcher widget at bottom-right corner.
- Natural language query understanding for legal questions and site navigation.
- **Direct Site Action Triggers**: Automatically opens SOS Mode, SP Complaint Generator, or State Directory directly from conversation buttons.
- Voice speech playback for AI answers.

### 🎴 6. Offline Citizen Rights Wallet Pass
- Pocket-sized summary card featuring top 5 mandatory constitutional & BNSS rights.
- Can be printed or saved directly to a phone's photo gallery for offline use when internet signal is unavailable.

### ⚡ 7. "Am I Being Detained?" Diagnostic Wizard
- 3-step rapid questionnaire wizard (*Location → Officer Action → Freedom Status*).
- Returns instant legal status classification, risk rating (Green / Yellow / Red), governing code sections, and direct action remedies.

### 🌐 8. Multilingual & Search Engine
- 7 Languages: **English**, **Hindi (हिंदी)**, **Marathi (मराठी)**, **Tamil (தமிழ்)**, **Bengali (বাংলা)**, **Telugu (తెలుగు)**, **Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)**.
- Real-time client-side search across legal keywords, section numbers, and scenarios.

## ⚖️ Legal Frameworks & Authoritative Citations

The application cites BOTH **New Criminal Laws (2023)** and **Old Acts** alongside landmark **Supreme Court of India Directives**:

| Legal Domain | New Law (2023) | Historical Act | Landmark SC Precedent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Criminal Procedure & Arrest** | Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) | CrPC 1973 | *D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal (1997)* |
| **Substantive Offenses** | Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) | Indian Penal Code (IPC) 1860 | *Arnesh Kumar v. State of Bihar (2014)* |
| **Evidence & Digital Proof** | Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) | Indian Evidence Act 1872 | *Paramvir Singh Saini v. Baljit Singh (2021)* |
| **Mandatory FIR Registration** | Sec 173(1) BNSS 2023 | Sec 154(1) CrPC 1973 | *Lalita Kumari v. Govt of U.P. (2014)* |
| **Women Safeguards & Arrest** | Sec 43(5) BNSS 2023 | Sec 46(4) CrPC 1973 | *Sheela Barse v. State of Maharashtra (1983)* |
| **Right to Privacy & Digital Search** | Sec 107 BNSS 2023 | Sec 102 CrPC 1973 | *K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (2017)* |
| **Anti-Corruption & Bribes** | Sec 7 PC Act 1988 / BNS 2023 | PC Act 1988 | *CBI v. Ashok Kumar Aggarwal (2014)* |

## 🚀 Local Setup & Installation Guide

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js**: v18.0.0 or higher
- **npm**: v9.0.0 or higher

### Steps

1. **Clone the Repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/SoumyaSagarNayak/knowthepolice.git
   cd knowthepolice
   ```

2. **Install Dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start Local Development Server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open your browser and navigate to **`http://localhost:3000/`**.

4. **Build Production Bundle**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
   Generates optimized production assets in `./dist`.

5. **Preview Production Build Locally**:
   ```bash
   npm run preview
   ```
